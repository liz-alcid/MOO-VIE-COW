**MOO-vie Cow**

**Submitted by:** Elizabeth Alcid (ealcid)

**Group Members:** Elizabeth Alcid (ealcid)

**Description:** MOO-vie Cow lets users search for any movie or TV show and automatically opens it on whichever streaming service it's available on (Netflix, Hulu, Max, Disney+, Prime Video, Paramount+, Apple TV+). Users can also maintain a personal watchlist and view their search history. Users must have an active account/subscription on the streaming service to watch inputted content.

**YouTube Video Link:** [MOO-vie Cow DEMO](https://youtu.be/NRooIoRhe0k)

**APIs:** [WatchMode API](https://api.watchmode.com/) — Streaming availability data for movies and TV shows
